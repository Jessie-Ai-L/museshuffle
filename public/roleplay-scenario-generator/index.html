Frequently asked questions

What should a roleplay scenario include?

A good scenario gives the players roles, a setting, a shared problem or objective, tension, and a clear starting line.

Is this only for tabletop RPGs?

No. It can also be used for character roleplay, improv, dialogue practice, creative writing, or scene exercises.
