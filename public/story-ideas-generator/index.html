Frequently asked questions

Is this a full story generator?

No. It creates a story seed: enough character, setting, conflict, and twist to start writing while leaving the actual story to you.

Can I use it for short stories?

Yes. The seeds work especially well for short fiction, writing exercises, classroom prompts, and brainstorming.
