Frequently asked questions

How is an art idea different from an art prompt?

An art idea is closer to a project concept: what to make and why it might be interesting. An art prompt is usually a more direct creative instruction.

Is this for AI art?

The generator is designed primarily for human artists and art practice, though you can adapt the ideas however you like.
