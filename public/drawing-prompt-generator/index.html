Frequently asked questions

What makes a good drawing prompt?

A useful drawing prompt gives enough direction to begin while leaving room for interpretation. Subject, setting, mood, style, and one constraint are usually enough.

Can I use these prompts for daily practice?

Yes. The generator is designed for sketchbooks, warmups, art challenges, classrooms, and personal practice.
